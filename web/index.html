A NOTE BEFORE YOU GO

Weather is a possibility.

Fographer helps you find promising conditions for ground and valley fog. Small pockets of mist can form between model grid cells and weather stations.

What the colors mean

Predicted fog: the model reports fog, or visibility is below 1 km with humidity at least 95% and precipitation below 0.2 mm/h.

Favorable conditions: humidity at least 95%, temperature within 2°C of dew point, wind at most 10 km/h, and precipitation below 0.2 mm/h. This is a heuristic, not a probability.

Station observations: separate measurements with their own timestamps. Low visibility alone does not confirm fog. Observations over 90 minutes old are marked stale and removed after three hours. Airport METAR reports provide observations across Europe; coverage between airports is limited. Germany also has DWD station observations. Visibility bounds such as ≥ are preserved.

Forecast sources are chosen automatically from the location. Germany uses ICON D2; other locations use DWD ICON Seamless, combining regional and global models. The Europe overview uses a coarse 2° grid. Zoom in for a finer sampled overlay; click a spot for its own model forecast. Overview cell size is not model resolution. Missing visibility is shown as unknown. “Now” shading is also a model estimate.

Offline, your saved spots and previously loaded forecasts remain available with their age shown. The basemap needs a connection.
